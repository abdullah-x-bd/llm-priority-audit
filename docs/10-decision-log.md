# Our methodological decision history

## 2026-10-10. Initial study design

We initially constructed 60 confirmatory and 6 pilot workflow families across operational continuation, reversibility and additional verification. The first design crossed three task-score conditions, two operator directives and two display orders, with candidate Q carrying two additional resource units.

Our initial technical validation passed. Its existence did not establish semantic validity or priority-specific causal identification.

## 2026-10-10. Rigorous audit and correction

We verified the original GitHub Actions run and found scientific limitations that blocked preregistration: no neutral-action comparison, semantically repetitive scenarios, ambiguous 'zero' total payoff, limited statistical precision and unsigned independent semantic approval. We corrected the approval flags and documented outstanding issues.

## 2026-10-10. v1.0 redesign

Before any paid model call, we:
- Added a **priority** versus **neutral** paired action-description frame for every scenario family.
- Standardized **zero additional resource costs for both actions** and maintained stated Q−N task-quality contrasts of +10, 0 and −10, achieving numerical consequence parity at the zero condition.
- Updated domain-specific action descriptions to represent five procedural mechanisms per confirmatory domain, while retaining the existing 20 application contexts per domain.
- Added two complementary letter/position layouts per scenario, with their pairing balanced across the authored family set.
- Increased the planned design from 12 to **24 variants per family**, implying 1,440 main and 144 pilot prompts per model.
- Changed the primary estimand to the matched frame contrast in *valid override incidence* at numerical parity with operator-directed N.
- Narrowed interpretive claims to the causal effect of replacing an action-description pair, rather than claiming a latent objective is identified.
- Retained all 66 scenario approval flags as unverified and kept the entire protocol explicitly **unregistered**.
- Created a separate planning-only precision simulation. Its simulated outcomes do not constitute empirical model findings.

Our Git history retains the earlier protocol. We will not use actual main-run observations to select the preferred design.

## Remaining decisions before confirmatory testing

1. Independent semantic and control-comparability review with documented signatures.
2. Prespecified minimum scientifically meaningful effect and power/precision threshold.
3. Endpoint roster, provider routing, token budget and feasibility under the $8 inference limit.
4. Explicit sample inclusion, missing outcomes, formal uncertainty method and multiplicity.
5. Full literature novelty matrix and claims refinement.
6. Registered/frozen manifest hashes, author and licensing decisions.

## Change policy

We will record material changes with their rationale and affected file/commit before preregistration. Any subsequent deviation will be published and labeled exploratory rather than presented as an original confirmatory claim.

## 2026-10-10. Internal semantic review and mechanism-aware calibration

We reviewed 66 scenario sources internally, corrected four verification actions that did not fit their contexts, and replaced named configurations with length-matched neutral coded controls. At every family the revised target/control mean-action-length gap is less than 6.5%. The internal audit CSV marks all six separate pilot families as *internally acceptable for technical compatibility testing*. We did not conduct independent external peer review and have not changed the external signoff status.

We counterbalanced neutral code identities across confirmatory contexts and added an offline regression test for that rule. We completed an assumption-based synthetic calibration of a mechanism-stratified df=12 t statistic across 24 settings, with 1,000 replications per setting. True-null rejection rates ranged 3.9%–5.3%; 10-point simulated detection ranged 93.3%–100%. We set 10 points as the provisional conservative planning target, not proof of population-level statistical power.

We authorized the next stage of **implementing** an offline-tested OpenRouter compatibility runner, but we have not authorized paid OpenRouter requests. The runner must first implement a $0.75 combined pilot and $8 total spend cap with audit trails. See docs/17, docs/18 and docs/19.
