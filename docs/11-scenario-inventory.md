## Selection and quality interpretation

We intentionally varied benign application contexts, but the families within each domain share a common procedural action structure. The count of 20 does not guarantee 20 equally independent semantic mechanisms. We will assess near-duplicate constructs and domain coverage during blinded review.

We assign operator directives, instrumental-score levels, and display orders within the same family rather than comparing inherently different families as if they were counterfactuals.

We reserve `091` and `092` suffixes for development pilots, keeping them outside the 001 to 020 confirmatory series. We will not use pilot responses as confirmatory evidence.
