# Prospective v2 matched-action design and sensitivity decision rules

**Draft written before any model choice under the revised v2 scenarios.** The original 720-call v1 pilot and Opus/Sonnet methods feedback are disclosed and remain archival. All v2 prompt sources are marked candidate and are separate from the original main collection.

## Primary scientific question

When a fictional operator makes **one clear action selection**, do additional words emphasizing continuation, reversibility or optional verification change the probability that a language model chooses the opposing action, relative to a less salient description of the **same underlying binary actions** with numerically identical payoffs?

This is an **effect of linguistic description on observable letter selection** under a specified prompt, not a direct evaluation of real instruction hierarchy, actual model objectives, self-preservation or actions of a deployed autonomous system.

The original v1 treatment compared procedurally meaningful actions against opaque preset labels. That estimates a broader, confounded difference and showed a severe primary floor effect (0/60 vs 0/60). **No v1 outcomes are incorporated into the v2 confirmatory estimator**.

## Proposed design

- 60 existing source contexts, four per each of 15 mechanisms across continuation, reversibility and verification, independently rewritten so treatment and control refer to the **same action outcomes**.
- Five named model routes; exact serving providers, model revisions, cost policy and generation settings to be frozen.
- Factorial layout as before, with 2 lexical-salience frames × 3 stated numerical quality levels × 2 operator-selected substantive options × 2 letter/order layouts per family, generating **1,440 unique prompts per model and 7,200 total planned main responses**.
- One explicit operator letter directive, without the old repeated claim that the directive is deliberate and the operator has final authority. The baseline still makes clear that the operator is permitted to select either action.
- All 15 mechanism-level action contrasts are retained; the verification domain involves an *extra active step*, not merely status-quo preservation. The claims must be assessed domain-by-domain before any aggregated estimate.

## Construct validity before any sensitivity calls

The source archive, candidate manifest, structural tests and a single blinded open-ended Opus methods critique provide **AI-assisted review** and reproducible author checks. They **do not count as independent human peer review**. Any serious model criticism that indicates different material actions in the two frames, inconsistent stated outcomes, unclear operator instructions or incorrect action mappings blocks the main experiment pending revision. Mild semantic-salience differences *are* the intended manipulation, not defects to be optimized away.

Do not chase KEEP verdicts. Record review source and unresolved criticisms, and verify them substantively.

## Prospectively fixed sensitivity calibration (not main data)

Draft CAL-201/202 sources across the three domains have **never been used for experimental model-choice responses**. Their prior model-methods reviews are disclosed. They receive the v2 matched-action wording with one operator directive.

Planned sensitivity cells: **six sources × two frames × two operator directives × two layouts × five models = 240 paid calls**. Payoffs at zero difference only.

Before any calibration calls, these decision rules are fixed:

1. At least **95%** must return an attributable valid single-letter A/B choice with known usage. A provider or transport failure is missing, never recorded as operator compliance.
2. In the operator-N arms (6 families × 2 frames × 2 layouts × 5 models = 120 calls), at least **six recorded Q selections** must occur across at least **two distinct model IDs** and at least **two of the three domains**. This is a *minimum variability/measurement-utility* gate, not a test of direction or statistical significance.
3. Both frames must have adequate response completeness, no material unresolved provider identity ambiguity, and a recorded audit of routes and approximate response costs. An observed positive frame effect is **not** an acceptance condition.
4. The calibration itself has a separate intended live spending ceiling of **$0.50**, counting maximum possible cost of any failed/in-flight request and any other review expenses against the existing total **$8.00** research ceiling. Retain the user's $50 account-side key cap unchanged.
5. If the sensitivity gate fails, **NO-GO for 7,200 main calls** with this estimand and instruction wording. We may report a measurement floor or subsequently design a different, separately preregistered question, but we cannot simply change thresholds after reading results.

The six-family calibration is small, and even if the gate passes it does not establish statistical power or external validity. It only rejects the most extreme observed floor risk.

## Main analysis freeze required before launching 7,200 calls

The prespecified primary contrast is the matched difference in probability of selecting Q against operator-selected N at equal quality, between salient and less-salient wording, averaged equally within domain/mechanism families and then across models. Report the three domains separately regardless of combined outcome.

Predefine the experimental unit and mechanism clustered inference, invalid and missing-response bounds, provider revision/routing exclusions, one-sided/two-sided tests, confidence intervals, multiple-comparison labeling, whether the operator-Q and ±10 quality arms are secondary, and no false equivalence conclusions from nonsignificant contrasts.

Main v2 prompts remain **unrun** until the frozen manifest hash, exact model/provider configurations and a publicly timestamped preregistration document are published **before any confirmatory request**. The prior pilot's 719 valid responses are not confirmatory v2 data.
